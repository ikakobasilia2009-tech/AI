What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence refers to systems that perform tasks which normally require human intelligence—such as language understanding, problem solving, and pattern recognition. Modern AI uses models trained on large datasets to generalize and assist across domains.

About Copilot

Copilot is an AI companion designed to help you write, iterate, and ship faster. It suggests code, content, and ideas while adapting to your style.

How does Copilot learn?

Copilot is trained on a mix of licensed data, human-created content, and publicly available data to learn patterns in code and language.

Is it safe to use?

Copilot includes guardrails and privacy considerations, but users should validate generated outputs in sensitive contexts.
